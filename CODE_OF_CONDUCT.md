# Code of conduct

photoc welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Help
keep this a respectful, practical place to work together.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing experiences and viewpoints, and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy. Share another person's information only with their permission.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project
spaces, and when representing photoc in public. Maintainers may remove content,
warn contributors, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently, taking
the severity and repeated behavior into account.

## Reporting

Contact a maintainer privately through a contact method they have published.
If no private contact is available, open an issue titled **Private conduct report
contact request** asking how to report privately. Include no incident details,
names, or personal information in that public request.

Maintainers will handle reports with discretion and share details only as needed
to address the incident. Maintainers are also expected to follow this policy.
